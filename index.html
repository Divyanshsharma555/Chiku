Chapter IV

A Letter For You

❤️

My Dearest Chiku,

Chiku, you are the best memory I have ever had in my life. Teri ankhen… unme kho jane ka man karta hai. Teri baatein toh poocho mat — aur tere hoth, jinse woh baat nikalti hai, unme toh doob jane ka man karta hai.

Teri pyaari si smile mujhe baar baar jeet leti hai. Aur toh aur, teri baaton mein interest na aaye — aisa kaise ho sakta hai bhala, Chikudaa? Tere saath bitaye har ek pal yaadgaar hai. Tere saath na hokar bhi, apne video calls se hum kitne connected hain.

Teri sundarta mujhe har roz teri ore kheenchti hai, aur mera pyaar tere liye har din badhta ja raha hai… har din. Pyaar bhi chhota shabd hai apne bond ke aage — isse ishq se bhi kuch badhkar hi kahenge.

Teri care karne mein mujhe maza aata hai. Tujhe bacche ki tarah rakhne mein maza aata hai. Tujhe sar aankhon pe rakhne mein maza aata hai.

And one more thing — my patent line, only for you:

“I love them only because they have half of u in them.”

Forever Yours,

❤️ Divyansh
